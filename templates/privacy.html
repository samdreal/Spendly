Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service to you. This includes:

  • Account information — such as your email address and a securely stored password, used to create and access your account.
  • Expense data — the transactions you add, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage information — limited technical details such as your general activity within the app, used to keep the Service working reliably.

We do not ask for or store your bank account numbers, card details, or other sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Display your expenses and generate summaries of your own spending patterns.
  • Secure your account and detect or prevent unauthorised access.
  • Respond to your questions and support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense entries for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored so that it can be retrieved when you sign in to the Service. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain your data for as long as your account is active, and you may request deletion of your account and its associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted third party services to operate — for example, hosting or infrastructure providers that store the Service's data. These providers only process information on our behalf and are expected to keep it secure.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Where the Service links to external websites, those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at privacy@spendly.app.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
